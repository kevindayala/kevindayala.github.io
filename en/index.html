Specialties

What I bring
to your team

Full-Stack Development

I hold my own on both sides: Laravel and PHP on the back end, React, Next.js, Vue, and TypeScript on the front end. If a feature needs to ship end to end, I can build it myself without waiting on another role to finish it.

Frontend & UX/UI

Beyond code, I come from a design background. I can start from a Figma file or build one myself, and land on an interface that's clear and usable. That helps when design and development aren't fully in sync.

Multi-tenant SaaS

I built AgendaCEA, a multi-tenant platform for driving schools, with per-client data isolation, CI/CD, and Docker deployment. It wasn't an architecture exercise — it ran in production.

DevOps & Infrastructure

I set up the environment with Docker, GitHub Actions, Cloudflare, and VPS hosting. I document deployments so the rest of the team can repeat them without guessing.

Business & requirements thinking

Before picking a tool, I make sure I understand the business problem. I talk to whoever has the need, translate it into clear criteria for the team, and propose something the business can actually maintain — not the most complex solution just to show off.

Automation & applied AI

I put language models into software people actually use: assistants, classification, content generation, and workflows that used to be manual. I keep up with what's new (including MCP) and automate with n8n and APIs when it makes sense.

Bonus: Digital Traffic & Analytics

I also run campaigns on Google Ads and Meta Ads, and track what happens next with GA4, Tag Manager, and Looker Studio. That way the landing page, the tracking, and the campaign are all thought through by the same person, and decisions come from numbers, not gut feeling.

Soft skills

Technical leadership

When I led the development team, I made the team's rules and delivery process clear. That way everyone knew what to build and how to get it production-ready.

Business communication

I talk to whoever has the problem, break it down into concrete points, and hand it to the team with no runaround or unnecessary jargon.

Product thinking

I favor whatever the business can actually maintain over time. If a simpler option solves it, that one wins, even if it looks less "impressive".

Problem solving

If something gets stuck (server, integration, or an AI model), I dig in and unblock it. I don't leave it hanging waiting for "someone else" to look at it.

Design & marketing collaboration

I move between Figma, code, and campaigns. I understand the interface, the tracking, and what gets measured after the click, so nothing gets lost between teams.

Data-driven

I check GA4, Ads, and Looker before forming an opinion. That way I know which landing page converts, which campaign performs, and what's worth touching first.
